**Associate Professor · School of Artificial Intelligence · Kookmin University**<br>
Principal Investigator, [Data Mining Lab.](https://dm.kookmin.ac.kr)

I am an Associate Professor in the [School of Artificial Intelligence](https://english.kookmin.ac.kr/academics/undergraduates/cs) at [Kookmin University](https://www.kookmin.ac.kr), South Korea, where I lead the Data Mining Lab. My research focuses on efficient data mining and machine learning, with an emphasis on scalable graph algorithms, high-dimensional similarity search, and AI model compression. Recent work includes neural network quantization and representation learning for satellite image and video retrieval.
